# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be respectful, constructive, and
accessible to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in issues, reviews, and other project spaces.
- Explain technical disagreements with evidence and address ideas rather than
  attacking people.
- Accept good-faith corrections and respect others' time and boundaries.
- Keep shared discussions relevant, and avoid posting private or identifying
  information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate intimidation, personal
attacks, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing another person's private
information are not acceptable. Repeated disruptive behavior may also result
in moderation.

## Enforcement and reporting

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
Report a concern privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub;
include relevant context, but do not post sensitive details publicly.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, and retaliation for
making a good-faith report is not acceptable.
